# Football fan-edit study ("text in the scene" style), 2026-11-11

A user-uploaded 27 s fan edit (768x576, 30 fps, stereo 44.1 kHz) was measured frame by frame for technique only.
It is made from TV match footage of a real player and a commercial song, so it is not recreated; the upload and
all frames were deleted after measuring (CLAUDE.md rule 6). Only these observations are kept.

| Technique | What the edit does |
|---|---|
| 3D word in the scene | One word, extruded and bevelled, placed in perspective in every shot and stuck to the scene as the camera moves. The subject passes in front of it, so a letter is hidden behind the body or framed by the head. |
| Word matches the shot | Colour follows the kit (blue, red, white, gold); seen from behind it reads mirrored; in the last shots it lies flat on the pitch. |
| Speed ramps | The key action (an overhead kick) plays at about 4-5x slow motion; the pose barely changes over 30 frames. |
| Rhythm | Music about 161 BPM (one beat every 0.372 s). Every shot lasts exactly 8 beats (2.98 s); shot changes land on the big hit, never a plain cut: zoom/blur and flash. |
| Inside a shot | A slow push-in, no cutting. |
| Look | Saturated teal/warm grade, soft glow on highlights, vignette, film grain. |
