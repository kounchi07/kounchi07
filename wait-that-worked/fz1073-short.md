# Short: flydubai FZ1073, the stabbed captain (about 50 seconds)

**Why this story:** more than 10 small channels got 1M–7M views on it within weeks. Several
had under 10K subscribers, and one had 2.3K.
**Facts checked against:** CBS News, CNBC, NBC News, CNN and the Jerusalem Post (links below).

## Script

September 30th, 2026. flydubai flight 1073, Dubai to Tel Aviv. 174 people on board.

Inside the cockpit, Captain Smit Machchhar is suddenly attacked by his own co-pilot. Stabbed.

Israeli officials say the co-pilot was trying to bring the plane down.

Bleeding, the captain does the one thing that can save everyone. He gets the cockpit door open.

Passengers and crew rush in and pin the attacker down.

But now nobody is flying the plane.

Two off-duty pilots, sitting in the cabin as passengers, take the controls and land the jet
in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia.

Every passenger made it off safely.

The captain was seriously injured. The co-pilot is being questioned.

One open door. 174 lives.

Would you have rushed the cockpit? Tell me below.

## Notes
- **Don't say the captain landed the plane.** Many viral versions say he did, but the
  reports say two off-duty pilots landed it. Getting this right protects you from "fake"
  comments.
- **Don't state a motive, religion or nationality for the attacker.** The investigation is
  still open, and reports disagree.
- **First second on screen:** the captain's 3D face with the text "STABBED BY HIS CO-PILOT".
